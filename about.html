About Audio Affairs

Audio Affairs is a premium sound solutions company based in Hyderabad, delivering exceptional audio experiences for events, concerts and corporate gatherings.

With professional equipment and a strong commitment to quality, we provide reliable audio, lighting and LED solutions for different types of live events.

Our work includes corporate events, movie and product promotions, club gigs, sporting events and social occasions.

Audio Affairs was founded by Y. Satya Chaitanya and TV Krishna Reddy, bringing years of experience in professional audio production to every event.
