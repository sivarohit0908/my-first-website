The people behind Audio Affairs

Our Founders

Built by sound engineers with years of experience across Hyderabad's live event and production industry.

Y. Satya Chaitanya

Co-Founder

Y. Satya Chaitanya began his journey as a trainee sound engineer at HICC with Highbrow Production Services in 2010.

Driven by a passion to create opportunities and deliver excellence in audio, he later joined hands with his close friend and fellow sound engineer TV Krishna Reddy.

In 2018, that shared vision became Audio Affairs. Since then, the company has worked across corporate events, movie and product promotions, club gigs, sporting events and social occasions.

TV Krishna Reddy

Co-Founder

TV Krishna Reddy began his audio career in 2004 with Planet Productions in Hyderabad.

With a strong passion for excellence, he wanted to create opportunities and redefine standards within the audio and event industry.

In 2018, he co-founded Audio Affairs with Y. Satya Chaitanya, building the company through consistent work across corporate events, promotional campaigns, club gigs, sporting events, product launches and social gatherings.
